# Introduction to Gravitational Waves and Pulsar Timing Array

A self-contained reading project introducing the physics of **pulsars**, **pulsar timing**, and **Pulsar Timing Arrays (PTAs)** as a method for detecting low-frequency gravitational waves. The report was completed as part of a supervised reading project at **Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Thiruvananthapuram** under **Dr. Abhimanyu Susobhanan**.

---

## Overview

This project develops the theoretical foundations required to understand how millisecond pulsars can be used as ultra-precise cosmic clocks for gravitational wave detection. Beginning with the basic physics of neutron stars, it gradually builds toward pulsar timing techniques and the principles behind Pulsar Timing Arrays.

The report emphasizes physical intuition alongside mathematical derivations, making it suitable for undergraduate students beginning research in astrophysics or gravitational-wave astronomy.

---

## Topics Covered

### Chapter 1 — Introduction to Pulsars

* Formation of neutron stars
* Pulsar emission and the lighthouse model
* Mass, density and radius estimates
* Magnetic dipole radiation
* Spin-down luminosity
* Characteristic age
* Braking index
* Pulsar evolution
* Emission mechanisms

### Chapter 2 — Pulsars and the Interstellar Medium

* Plasma propagation of radio waves
* Dispersion measure (DM)
* Dispersion delay
* Scatter broadening
* Coherent dedispersion

### Chapter 3 — Pulsar Timing

* Pulse Time of Arrival (TOA)
* Timing precision
* Timing corrections
* Solar-system and binary corrections
* Pulsar Timing Arrays (PTAs)
* Hellings–Downs correlation
* Fourier-domain determination of pulse arrival times
* Goodness-of-fit approach for TOA estimation

These topics form the progression of the report from basic pulsar physics to modern gravitational-wave detection techniques.
